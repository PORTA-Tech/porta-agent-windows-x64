## 8. New — the late start hole

Found 25 August. Chat 2 added an 11:00 ceiling on the morning card. A day that starts
after 11:00 therefore gets no morning card at all, so no tasks are captured, so the
evening card has no board and the day records nothing. A noon start is a silent day.

**Decision:** when work starts after 11:00, the first card of the day is still a plan
card, worded for a short day rather than a full one. Do not move the 11:00 ceiling and do
not remove it. The ceiling exists to stop a card about the day ahead arriving when there
is no day ahead; a late start still has a day ahead, just a shorter one.
