The weekly, and how it differs from the daily

The daily answers how did today go. The weekly answers did the week go where I meant it to, so it is built from days and projects rather than from hours and apps. Same tokens, same green rule: green means work that counted, nothing else.

The week strip replaces the daily timeline. One bar per day, filled to focused time. A day not worked is an empty track, never a zero bar, because a gap and a bad day are not the same thing. Under three worked days the strip is replaced by a sentence, same rule the client report follows.

Finished and carried are separate blocks. Finished is the week's evidence. Carried names how many days each thing has been open, which is the only number that tells you something is stuck rather than slow.

Where the week went is by project, not by app. On a single day the app split is a useful mirror. Across a week it says nothing you can act on, and the project split says everything. Apps stay on the daily.

Ari's take is the same serif voice as the daily, drafted from result lines and statuses only, never from app usage.

Every column carries its width inline as well as as an attribute, and every bar carries its proportion inline, so this survives Gmail iOS stripping attributes — the failure fixed on the daily this afternoon.

One thing worth deciding separately: your phone renders the daily card light, not dark. Gmail inverts it. The mockup is the dark original.
